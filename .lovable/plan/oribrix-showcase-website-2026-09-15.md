# Oribrix showcase website

## Goal

Build a complete, responsive Oribrix website that presents the company as a connected B2B construction-material procurement and fulfillment platform.

## Pages

- **Home:** Brand-led introduction, material search/category showcase, benefits, three-sided platform overview, and strong enquiry call-to-action.
- **Products:** Swiggy-inspired browsing patterns adapted for construction materials: location context, search, category tiles, popular materials, and bulk/RFQ pathways.
- **Solutions:** Dedicated customer, vendor, and operations/admin experiences with their role-specific workflows.
- **How it works:** A clear journey from site setup and material discovery through ordering, fulfillment, tracking, invoices, and support.
- **About:** Oribrix mission, operating model, and the value of one connected supply network.
- **Contact:** Practical sales/procurement enquiry form with transparent placeholder contact details where source material is unavailable.

## Visual direction

- Use the Oribrix electric blue from the uploaded logo as the primary brand color.
- Follow Neutomni’s structural and visual language: clean editorial spacing, confident oversized typography, crisp grids, restrained motion, and modern enterprise polish.
- Use Swiggy only as a reference for discoverability: prominent search, clear categories, fast product scanning, and location-aware service presentation.
- Keep surfaces bright and high-contrast with graphite text, white space, blue brand bands, and small warm accent moments; avoid copying Swiggy’s colors or design.
- Use the supplied Oribrix logo prominently and create a matching favicon.

## Content and interactions

- Translate the SRS into customer-facing language without exposing technical requirements or internal implementation detail.
- Present core categories such as cement, steel, aggregates, blocks, electrical, plumbing, and finishing materials.
- Include product search/filter interactions, category selection, an RFQ/bulk-order call-to-action, mobile navigation, FAQ expansion, and validated contact form feedback.
- Showcase operational trust signals: site-specific availability, bulk pricing, live order stages, verified vendors, proof of delivery, and invoice visibility.

## Technical details

- Build with the existing React/TanStack structure and shared page chrome.
- Create a separate route for each major page, each with unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Define all color, typography, spacing, and motion choices as semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Store the uploaded logo through the project asset flow, while making a separate square favicon copy.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, interactive states, form behavior, and browser console output.

## Assumptions

- This is a showcase and lead-generation website, not the production procurement application itself.
- No customer account, checkout, live inventory, payment, or backend persistence is included in this phase.
- Phone number, email, office address, coverage cities, prices, and business metrics will not be invented; any unavailable contact data will be clearly marked for replacement.
